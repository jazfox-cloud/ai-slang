What does Muse mean?

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent, designed to work toward a person's goals across connected services from a dedicated cloud environment called Muse Secure VM.

Muse is Meta's name for a personal agent that can keep working toward an ongoing goal. It has its own controlled computer environment, but the person decides what access it receives and when an approval is needed.

Origin and usage

Meta introduced Muse in September 2026 as a personal agent that can turn goals into plans and take approved actions, rather than only returning chat responses.

Source type: product-term. Last checked: 2026-10-06.

Product term verified against Meta's launch material. Muse's country availability, connected services, purchasing features, and the actions it can take depend on the current product rollout and the access a person chooses to grant.

Muse versus a chat assistant

A chat assistant mainly answers within a conversation. Meta describes Muse as a personal agent that can retain goal context, prepare a plan, and work through connected services on a person's behalf.

Muse is not the same as a general chat assistant because it is designed for continuing work, not only a single conversational response. That does not mean Muse has unlimited authority: its usefulness depends on the services a person connects and the approvals that the product requires for a particular action.

What is Muse Secure VM?

Muse Secure VM is Meta's name for the dedicated virtual-machine environment where Muse works. Meta presents it as a separate computer for the agent, its browser activity, and the data needed for a task.

A secure VM is an implementation and permission boundary, not a guarantee that every connected account or action is risk-free. People still need to decide what access to grant and review actions that matter.

What can change

  • Product access, availability, and connected services can change.
  • Which actions are automatic and which require approval.
  • Model names, subscription offers, and safety controls.

Examples

  • I asked Muse to organize the trip, then it came back when a booking needed my approval.
  • Muse is meant to work toward a goal over time, not just answer one prompt.

FAQ

What is Muse?

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent. Meta says it can help people turn goals into plans and work through connected services from a dedicated environment called Muse Secure VM.

Is Muse the same as a general chat assistant?

No. A chat assistant is mainly a conversational interface, while Muse is designed to keep context for goals and carry out approved multi-step work across connected services.

Does Muse act without approval?

The exact approval flow depends on the task and product rollout. Meta says people control how much access Muse receives, so users should review the current product controls before connecting accounts or authorizing actions.

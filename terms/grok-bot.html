What does Grok Bot mean?

Grok Bot is xAI's product for persistent AI teammates that work on a persistent cloud computer with a browser, filesystem, and terminal, then return when a person needs to review or approve something.

Grok Bot is xAI's name for an AI teammate that can retain job context and work in a persistent cloud environment. It is more than the normal Grok chat interface, but it still needs the permissions and approvals a person gives it.

Origin and usage

xAI launched Grok Bot in 2026 as a separate product category from the standard Grok assistant and from Grok Build, positioning Bots as named teammates with jobs and context that compounds over time.

Grok Bot versus the standard Grok assistant

The standard Grok assistant is a general-purpose conversational product. Grok Bot is xAI's product for named AI teammates that can retain job context and work through a persistent cloud environment.

The product distinction matters because a Bot can be assigned a continuing role and tools. It is not just another name for any Grok chat response.

Grok Bot versus Grok Build

Grok Build is xAI's coding-agent harness and terminal-focused development tool. Grok Bot is the broader teammate product for multi-step work across a Bot's cloud computer and connected tools.

They can overlap in agentic behavior, but they are different products with different workflows. A coding task might suit Grok Build; a persistent named teammate with a broader job is the Grok Bot concept.

What can change

  • Product platform support and subscription eligibility can change.
  • Connected tools, browser behavior, and approval steps.
  • The Bot's available model, features, and work limits.

FAQ

What is Grok Bot?

Grok Bot is xAI's product for persistent AI teammates. xAI says each Bot can work on a cloud computer with a browser, filesystem, and terminal, then return when approval or input is needed.

Is Grok Bot the same as Grok Build?

No. Grok Build is xAI's coding-agent harness and terminal tool, while Grok Bot is the broader persistent-teammate product for multi-step work across connected tools.

Can Grok Bot act without review?

The available actions and approval flow depend on the current product, connected tools, and permissions. Review xAI's current controls before granting a Bot access to accounts or consequential actions.
